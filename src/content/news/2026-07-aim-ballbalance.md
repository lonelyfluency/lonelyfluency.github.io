"Sim-to-Real Reinforcement Learning for Ball-Balancing Locomotion on
Quadruped Robots" (with Hamidreza Raei, Arash Ajoudani, and Panos Trahanias)
was presented at the IEEE/ASME International Conference on Advanced
Intelligent Mechatronics in Genova — a Unitree Go2 walks while keeping a
ball balanced on a plate on its back.
