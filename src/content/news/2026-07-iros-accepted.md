"Role-Based Reward Decomposition for Legged Locomotion Reinforcement
Learning" (with Panos Trahanias) has been accepted at IEEE/RSJ IROS 2026 —
partitioning locomotion rewards by stance and swing roles stabilizes
multi-objective learning, validated on a real Unitree Go2 and extended to
the G1 biped. I will present it at the conference this September.
